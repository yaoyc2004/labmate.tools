SHEET GEOMETRY · MM

Top and left are measured to the edge of the first label. Pitch is from one label’s left (or top) edge to the next one’s — measure across 5 labels and divide by 5. Editing any value switches to a custom sheet, saved in this browser.

03 — ALIGN TO YOUR PRINTER

  1. Print the calibration page on plain paper.
  2. Hold it over a blank label sheet against the light.
  3. Enter how far to move the print so the crosses land on the label centres.

Positive X moves the print right, positive Y moves it down. Top-left correct but bottom-right off? The spacing is wrong, not the offset — fix the pitch under Sheet geometry.

Worked example

Example. 96 tubes, IDs S-0001 to S-0096, on Diversified Biotech LCRY-1700 sheets (85 labels, 5 × 17): you need 2 sheets — 85 labels on the first and 11 on the second, leaving 74 blank labels for the next run. With 2 copies per ID it is 192 labels, or 3 sheets.

QUESTIONS · CRYO LABEL MAKER

Frequently asked

Which printers and label sheets does it work with?

Laser printers with laser cryogenic label sheets. You can pick a sheet or enter its geometry. It is not made for thermal label printers.

How do I line the labels up with my printer?

Print the calibration page on plain paper, hold it over a blank label sheet against the light, and enter how far to move the print. If the top-left lines up but the bottom-right doesn't, fix the pitch under Sheet geometry instead.

Can I paste sample IDs from Excel or a CSV file?

Yes — paste one ID per line, or open a .csv file (the first column is used). The file is read on your computer and never uploaded.

What print settings should I use?

Set the scale to Actual size / 100%, not Fit to page, and feed each label sheet through the printer only once.

Which cryo label sheets are built in?

Diversified Biotech LCRY-1700, LCRY-2380, LCRY-1200, LCRY-1100 and LCRY-1258, LabTAG CL-23 and CL-70 on US Letter, and LabTAG A4RCL-23 and A4RCL-6 on A4. Any other sheet works by entering its label size and grid.

How many labels fit on one sheet?

From 30 labels (2.625 × 1 in, LCRY-1258) to 126 (1.28 × 0.5 in, LabTAG CL-23). The tool shows how many sheets you need and how many labels stay blank on the last one.

Can I put a QR code on each cryo label?

Yes. Pick the ID + QR template: each label gets the sample ID as text and as a QR code you can scan from the tube.

Does it work with A4 label sheets?

Yes. Two A4 sheets are built in, and any A4 or Letter sheet can be set up by entering its geometry.
