    ABOUT THIS TOOL

    How to use

    1. Enter your rotor radius in cm or mm. It is usually printed on the rotor or its lid, or listed in the centrifuge manual.
    2. Protocol says “spin at 12,000 × g”? Choose SOLVE FOR RPM and type 12000 in the force box.
    3. Centrifuge only shows rpm and you want to know the force? Choose SOLVE FOR × g.
    4. Protocol written in rpm for someone else's centrifuge? Use Match another centrifuge: enter their rpm and their rotor radius to get the rpm that gives the same force on yours.
    5. Many centrifuges can also be switched to show × g (RCF) directly — check the display settings.

    The formula

    RCF = 1.118 × 10⁻⁵ × r × rpm² rpm = √( RCF ÷ (1.118 × 10⁻⁵ × r) )
    RCFrelative centrifugal force, in × g
    rrotor radius in cm
    rpmrevolutions per minute

    Example. 12,000 × g with an 8.4 cm rotor: rpm = √(12000 ÷ (1.118 × 10⁻⁵ × 8.4)) ≈ 11,300 rpm.

    Good to know

    1. Free to use. No subscription. Nothing you type leaves your browser.
    2. Force grows with the square of speed: double the rpm and you get four times the × g.
    3. Never run a rotor above its rated maximum speed, whatever the calculation says.
    4. Speeds are rounded to the nearest 10 rpm; forces to the nearest whole × g.
