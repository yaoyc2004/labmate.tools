    Text size guide

    TextSuggested printed size
    Title72 pt
    Section headings36 pt

    Printed size = the size in your file × the print scale. These are guides for reading at about 1–2 m; your conference may set its own rules.

    Worked example

    Example. A poster laid out at 36 × 48 in but printed on A0 portrait (33.1 × 46.8 in) is scaled to 92%, so 24 pt body text prints at about 22 pt and is flagged. A 1,200-pixel-wide photo printed 8 in wide has 150 ppi (pass); stretched to 16 in it has 75 ppi and will print blurry.

    QUESTIONS · POSTER CHECK

    Frequently asked

    Is my poster file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The file is read on your computer and never uploaded, so it is safe for unpublished work.

    Which file types can it check?

    PDF, PowerPoint (PPTX), PNG and JPG.

    What does Poster Check look at?

    Whether your file's shape and size match the print size you will order and fit the board limit from the conference page, plus image resolution and text size at full print size. You get a checklist PDF and an email for the print shop.

    What doesn't it check?

    Colour mode and profile, bleed, transparency and overprint, and spelling. A browser can't judge these reliably, so they go on the checklist as questions for the print shop.

    What resolution do poster images need?

    About 150 pixels per inch at the printed size. Poster Check passes images at 150 ppi or more, warns between 100 and 149, and flags anything under 100 ppi as likely to print blurry.

    What font size should text be on a research poster?

    A common guide is 72 pt for the title, 36 pt for section headings, 24 pt for body text and 18 pt for captions, at the printed size. Poster Check flags body text under 24 pt and any text under 12 pt.

    What are standard conference poster sizes?

    In North America 48 × 36 in (landscape) and 36 × 48 in (portrait) are most common; in Europe A0 (841 × 1189 mm). Always use the size and board limit on your conference's page.

    Will my 16:9 PowerPoint slide fit a 48 × 36 in board?

    Not without blank bands: 16:9 is wider than 4:3. Printed 48 in wide it is 27 in tall. Poster Check shows the fit and lets you keep the bands white or trim them.
