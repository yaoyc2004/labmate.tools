Good to know

  1. Spillover here is a theoretical estimate from spectra and your filters. Real values also depend on detector voltages and laser power, so compensation always comes from single-stain controls on your instrument; spreading error is not modelled.
  2. Measured spectra: FPbase (CC BY-SA 4.0). Dyes not on FPbase are modelled from their published peak wavelengths.
  3. Instrument presets are published default or core-facility configurations. Facilities often swap filters — check the labels on your instrument and use EDIT FILTERS.
  4. Vendor-neutral: dyes from every supplier are listed side by side; nothing is sponsored. Peak values come from each maker's published tables.

Frequently asked

Does the spillover estimate replace compensation?

No. Spillover here is a theoretical estimate from spectra and your filters. Real values also depend on detector voltages and laser power, so compensation always comes from single-stain controls on your instrument. Spreading error is not modelled.

My cytometer's filters differ from the preset. What can I do?

Facilities often swap filters. Check the labels on your instrument and use EDIT FILTERS, or add your own instrument.

Is the dye list sponsored by a vendor?

No. Dyes from every supplier are listed side by side, with peak values from each maker's published tables. Nothing is sponsored.

Where are my panels saved?

In your browser. Clearing site data or switching browsers loses them, so export a copy if they matter. You can also share a panel by link.
