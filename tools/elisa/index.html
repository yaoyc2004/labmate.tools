    ABOUT THIS TOOL

    How to use

    1. Export the plate from the reader software — the 8×12 plate view or a list of wells — and open it here, or paste the block from Excel. When there are several reads (450 and 570/630 nm), pick the signal and the reference to subtract.
    2. Set the layout: pick a common layout, or choose STANDARD, SAMPLE, CONTROL or BLANK, the number of replicates and the direction, and tap the first well of each. Tap EXCLUDE then a well to leave a bad well out.
    3. Type the standard concentrations (or the top and the fold), the dilution of each sample and the expected value of any control.
    4. Check the curve: the fit, the back-calculated standards and the quantification range. AUTO picks 4PL or 5PL and says why.
    5. Read the results: each flag comes with the next step. Print the report (or save it as PDF), take the CSV, and SAVE RECORD to reopen the same analysis later.

    The formula

    y = D + (A − D) ÷ [1 + (x ÷ C)B]G
    AOD at zero concentration
    DOD at saturation
    Cinflection point (EC50 for 4PL)
    B, Gslope; asymmetry (G = 1 is 4PL)
    xC · {[(A − D) ÷ (y − D)]1/G − 1}1/B × dilution

    Example. 4PL with A = 0.05, B = 1.2, C = 150 pg/mL, D = 3.0. A sample diluted 1:10 reads OD 1.20: (A − D) ÷ (y − D) = −2.95 ÷ −1.80 = 1.639; 1.639 − 1 = 0.639; 0.6391/1.2 = 0.688; × 150 = 103.3 pg/mL in the well, × 10 = 1033 pg/mL.

    Good to know

    1. Free, no account, not tied to any kit or reader brand. Files are read in your browser and never uploaded; the last analysis stays on this device until you press CLEAR.
    2. Concentrations come from the mean OD of the replicates; each well's own value is in the working and the CSV.
    3. The tool never drops a well by itself and never reports an extrapolated value as a result.

    使用步骤

    1. 从读板软件导出整板数据（8×12 板式或孔号列表），在这里打开，或者从 Excel 直接粘贴。如果有多个波长（450 和 570/630 nm），选好信号读数和要减去的参比读数。
    2. 设定板布局：选一个常用布局，或者选“标准品 / 样本 / 质控 / 空白”、复孔数和方向，然后点每组的第一个孔。选“排除”再点某个孔，可以把坏孔剔除。
    3. 填入标准品浓度（或者最高浓度和倍比）、每个样本的稀释倍数，以及质控的预期浓度。
    4. 检查标准曲线：拟合参数、标准品回算和定量范围。“自动”会在 4PL 和 5PL 之间选择并说明理由。
    5. 查看结果：每个标记都附有下一步该怎么做。可以打印报告（或另存为 PDF）、导出 CSV，并用保存记录在以后重新打开同一份分析。

    公式

    y = D + (A − D) ÷ [1 + (x ÷ C)B]G
    A浓度为零时的 OD
    D饱和时的 OD
    C拐点（4PL 时即 EC50）
    B, G斜率；不对称因子（G = 1 即 4PL）
    xC · {[(A − D) ÷ (y − D)]1/G − 1}1/B × 稀释倍数

    例子。4PL 参数 A = 0.05，B = 1.2，C = 150 pg/mL，D = 3.0。某样本 1:10 稀释后 OD 为 1.20：(A − D) ÷ (y − D) = −2.95 ÷ −1.80 = 1.639；1.639 − 1 = 0.639；0.6391/1.2 = 0.688；× 150 = 孔内 103.3 pg/mL，× 10 = 1033 pg/mL。

    须知

    1. 免费、免登录，不绑定任何试剂盒或酶标仪品牌。文件在浏览器里读取，从不上传；最近一次分析保存在本机，按“清空”即删除。
    2. 浓度由复孔的平均 OD 反算；每个孔各自的值在计算过程和 CSV 里。
    3. 工具不会自动剔除任何孔，也不会把外推值当作结果报告。

    QUESTIONS · ELISA STANDARD CURVE ANALYZER

    Frequently asked

    Should I fit my ELISA standard curve with 4PL or 5PL?

    Start with 4PL. 5PL adds an asymmetry parameter and helps when the curve bends differently at the top and bottom, but it needs more standard levels and can fit noise. The tool fits both, uses 5PL only when an F-test says it fits significantly better and its parameters are well determined, and tells you why.

    Why weight the fit by 1/Y²?

    ELISA replicates scatter more at high OD than at low OD. An unweighted fit lets the top standards dominate and the low end, where most samples sit, fits worst. Weighting each point by 1/Y² (or 1/Y) evens that out. The weights here come from the mean OD of each standard level (at least 1% of the OD span), so the result can be reproduced in R or Prism.

    How are LLOQ and ULOQ decided?

    Each standard is read back off the fitted curve. The quantification range is the widest run of neighbouring standards that recover 80–120% of their nominal concentration with a CV of at most 20%, extended by one level at either end if that level is within 75–125% and 25% — the acceptance limits for ligand-binding assays in FDA and ICH M10 guidance. ALL STANDARDS uses the lowest and highest standard instead.

    What do I do with a sample above the top standard?

    Don't report the extrapolated number. The tool flags it > ULOQ and tells you how much more to dilute it so it lands in the middle of the range, for example “dilute 5× more, re-run at 1:50”. A saturated well can't be estimated at all, so the advice is a minimum.

    Should I subtract the zero standard?

    Either fit the zero standard as a point on the curve (the default) or subtract its mean OD from every well; both are common, so follow your kit insert. If the plate has substrate-only blank wells, subtract those.

    Does it work for competitive ELISA?

    Yes. The curve direction is detected from the standards (signal falling with concentration), %B/B0 is shown against the zero standard, and the limit of detection is taken below the zero-standard signal.

    Is my data uploaded?

    No. Files are read and fitted in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere and there is no account. The last analysis stays on this device until you press CLEAR.

    常见问题 · ELISA 标准曲线分析

    常见问题

    标准曲线用 4PL 还是 5PL？

    先用 4PL。5PL 多了一个不对称参数，曲线上下两端弯曲程度不同时更贴合，但需要更多的标准品浓度点，也更容易拟合到噪声。工具会同时拟合两种模型，只有当 F 检验显示 5PL 明显更好、且参数估计稳定时才采用 5PL，并说明理由。

    为什么用 1/Y² 加权？

    ELISA 复孔在高 OD 处的离散度比低 OD 处大。不加权时高浓度标准品主导拟合，而大多数样本所在的低浓度端反而拟合得最差。按 1/Y²（或 1/Y）给每个点加权可以平衡这一点。这里的权重取自每个标准品浓度点的平均 OD（下限为 OD 跨度的 1%），因此结果可以在 R 或 Prism 里复现。

    LLOQ 和 ULOQ 怎么确定？

    把每个标准品代回拟合曲线反算浓度。定量范围是回收率在标示浓度 80–120%、CV 不超过 20% 的最长一段连续标准品；如果两端相邻的浓度点在 75–125%、CV 25% 以内，再各向外扩展一个点——这是 FDA 和 ICH M10 对配体结合分析的接受标准。选择“全部标准品”则直接用最低和最高标准品。

    样本超过最高标准品怎么办？

    不要报告外推值。工具会标记为 > ULOQ，并告诉你需要再稀释多少倍才能落在定量范围中段，例如“再稀释 5 倍，按 1:50 重测”。饱和孔完全无法估算浓度，所以给出的是最低稀释倍数。

    零浓度标准品要不要扣除？

    可以把零浓度标准品作为曲线上的一个点参与拟合（默认），也可以从每个孔中减去它的平均 OD；两种做法都很常见，请按试剂盒说明书。如果板上有只加底物的空白孔，则扣除空白孔。

    支持竞争法 ELISA 吗？

    支持。曲线方向由标准品自动判断（信号随浓度下降），并以零浓度标准品为基准显示 %B/B0，检测限取在零浓度标准品信号之下。

    数据会上传吗？

    不会。文件在浏览器中读取和拟合，不会发送到任何地方，也不需要账号。最近一次分析保存在本机，按“清空”即删除。
