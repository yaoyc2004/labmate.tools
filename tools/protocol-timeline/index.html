    ABOUT THIS TOOL

    How to use

    1. Pick a TEMPLATE or start blank. Tap any step to change its time, the shortest and longest it may take, and the minutes you are needed at its start and end.
    2. Under WHEN, choose START AT or DONE BY and the time, and set your working day.
    3. Under GROUPS, set how many groups, how many people, and each group’s buffer. Leave STAGGER on AUTO to get clash-free start times.
    4. Read the TIMELINE: when to do what, clashes and after-hours steps, and when to leave and come back for overnight steps.
    5. At the bench, press START BENCH MODE and press DONE as you finish each step; the rest of the plan moves with you. Add the calendar file too, for alarms while the phone is locked.

    How it plans

    step = time + window + hands-on minutes
    Hands-onyou are busy for the whole step
    Waityou are needed for a few minutes at the start and end; the time may stretch between its shortest and longest
    Washesn equal changes, a minute or two of you at each
    Staggereach group starts at the earliest time, on the rounding grid, where its hands-on minutes plus its buffer meet no more groups than you have people
    Working daya hands-on minute at night stretches the nearest flexible step before it, never past its longest time

    DONE BY works backwards from the deadline minus the minutes kept free, then plans forwards from the start it finds.

    Example. Western blot, 1 group, DONE BY Thursday 12:00, workday 8:30–18:00, 15 min kept free, 10% step buffer: start Wednesday 12:10, primary on at 17:22 for 16 h, back Thursday 9:22 for the washes, last image done at 11:42. Start it at 9:00 instead and the planner warns that even 18 h of primary ends at 8:12, before your day starts.

    Good to know

    1. Free to use. Nothing leaves your browser.
    2. Template times are typical, not your kit’s. Check them and save your own version.
    3. Hands-on minutes decide the stagger: a 3 × 10 min wash needs you for a minute at each change, not for 30 minutes.
    4. Bench mode keeps the screen on and beeps while the page is open. Calendar alarms ring when the phone is locked; a web page can’t.

    QUESTIONS · PROTOCOL PLANNING

    Frequently asked

    How do I work out when to start my western blot?

    Pick the Western blot template, choose DONE BY and the time you want the blot imaged. The planner works backwards through every step, including the overnight primary, and shows the latest start time that still keeps the hands-on steps inside your working day.

    Can I plan several groups or samples that start at different times?

    Yes. Set the number of groups and leave STAGGER on AUTO. Each group starts as early as it can without needing you while another group does, so washes, changes and additions never land on top of each other. With two people at the bench, choose 2 under PEOPLE.

    What is the per-group buffer?

    A guard band in minutes after each hands-on part of that group. When groups are staggered, the next group keeps that much distance, so if this group runs a few minutes late it does not bump into the next one. Turn it on or off and set the minutes per group.

    Why did the planner make my incubation longer?

    Many steps have a window, such as blocking for 1–2 h or a primary overnight for 12–18 h. When a hands-on step would fall at night or clash with another group, the planner stretches the nearest flexible step inside its window so the next step starts in your working day. It says which step it stretched and by how much. It never goes past the longest time you set.

    What happens if a step runs late?

    In BENCH MODE, press DONE when you really finish a step. The next step starts then, the step buffers take up the delay first, and a flexible wait such as blocking for 1–2 h shortens or lengthens inside its window to bring you back on plan. Whatever is left moves the rest of that group, and the page shows it, for example +10 min.

    Will my phone ring when the next step is due?

    Two ways. BENCH MODE keeps the screen on, counts down each step, and beeps and buzzes when you are needed, as long as the page is open. ADD TO CALENDAR saves a calendar file with every step and an alarm 5 minutes before each; your calendar app rings even when the phone is locked, which a web page cannot do.

    Are the template times right for my kit?

    They are typical times for a common version of each protocol, checked against the public protocols linked under each template. Your antibody, kit or cell line may differ: tap a step to change its time, window or hands-on minutes, then SAVE AS MY TEMPLATE.

    Is anything uploaded?

    No. The plan is worked out in your browser and kept in this browser only. Export MY TEMPLATES to move them to another device.
