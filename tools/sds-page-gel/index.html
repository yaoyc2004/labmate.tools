    ABOUT THIS TOOL

    How to use

    1. Pick your PLATES. For anything other than a Bio-Rad Mini-PROTEAN, choose Other plates and enter the inside width and height of the glass and the spacer thickness.
    2. Under RESOLVING GELS, enter the percentage and the number of gels. Pouring two percentages at once? Add a row for each.
    3. Set OVERAGE for what stays in the tube and pipette (10% is usual) and, if you like, round each mix up to a volume that is easy to measure.
    4. Once, open YOUR STOCKS and set your acrylamide (30% or 40%), Tris, SDS and APS stocks. They are kept in this browser for next time.
    5. Follow each recipe card from the top and tick the components off. Add APS and TEMED last, swirl gently, and pour straight away.
    6. Overlay the resolving gel with water or isopropanol while it sets. Pour the stacking gel once it has, then insert the comb.

    The formula

    Vi = V × Ci, final ÷ Ci, stock
    Vmix volume = gels × cassette × share × (1 + overage)
    Vivolume of acrylamide, Tris, SDS, APS or TEMED stock
    waterV minus every other component

    The acrylamide:bis ratio is a label: it changes how the gel sieves, not the volumes.

    Example. 10 mL of 10% resolving gel from 30% acrylamide: 10 × 10 ÷ 30 = 3.33 mL acrylamide, 10 × 0.375 ÷ 1.5 = 2.5 mL 1.5 M Tris, 0.1 mL 10% SDS, 50 µL 10% APS, 5 µL TEMED, and 4.01 mL water.

    Good to know

    1. Free to use. Nothing you type leaves your browser.
    2. Unpolymerised acrylamide is a neurotoxin. Wear gloves and handle the stock in a fume hood or as your lab requires.
    3. The resolving share is an estimate (75% leaves about 1.5–2 cm for the stacking gel and comb on a mini gel). Change it if you pour more or less.
    4. A calculator, not a protocol: check against your lab's method.

    QUESTIONS · SDS-PAGE GEL RECIPE

    Frequently asked

    How much gel do I need for one mini gel?

    A Bio-Rad Mini-PROTEAN cassette holds 4.2 mL at 0.75 mm, 5.6 mL at 1.0 mm and 8.4 mL at 1.5 mm in all. The page gives about three quarters of that to the resolving gel and the rest to the stacking gel, and you can change the share.

    How do I make a 10% resolving gel?

    For 10 mL with 30% acrylamide/bis: 3.33 mL acrylamide, 2.5 mL 1.5 M Tris-HCl pH 8.8, 0.1 mL 10% SDS, 50 µL 10% APS and 5 µL TEMED, with water to 10 mL (4.01 mL).

    Why does my recipe sheet say 4.1 mL of water and this page 4.01 mL?

    Printed recipes usually leave APS and TEMED out of the water sum, so the mix ends slightly over 10 mL. Switch water to AS PRINTED to match them; EXACT TOTAL makes the mix exactly the volume shown.

    Can I use 40% acrylamide?

    Yes. Set the acrylamide stock to 40% under YOUR STOCKS; every volume follows. The ratio (29:1, 37.5:1, 19:1) is only a label and does not change the volumes.

    How do I pour two different percentages at once?

    Add a row under RESOLVING GELS for each percentage with its number of gels. Each gets its own resolving mix; one stacking mix covers all of them.

    My gel did not polymerize. What should I check?

    Most often the APS is old. Make 10% APS fresh or use frozen single-use aliquots, check the TEMED, and add both last, just before pouring.
