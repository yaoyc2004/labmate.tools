      ABOUT THIS TOOL

      How to use

      1. Enter the concentration you want in the first tube — the top of your series or standard curve.
      2. Pick the fold per step (2× for a half-dilution, 10× for a log series) and how many tubes.
      3. Enter how much you need left in each tube for your assay.
      4. If the top tube must itself be made from a stronger stock, enter the stock. The plan adds that step.
      5. Follow the steps: diluent into every tube first, then transfer and mix from tube to tube. Change tips between tubes.
      6. COPY PLAN pastes into Excel or your notebook; PRINT gives a one-page bench sheet.

      The formula

      Ck = C1 ÷ Fk−1
      transferT = V ÷ (F − 1)
      diluentD = V (tubes 2 to n)
      tube 1V + T of the top concentration

      Each tube gets D of diluent plus T from the tube before: (T + D) ÷ T = F.

      Example. 6 tubes, 2-fold, 100 µL each from 1000 ng/mL: put 100 µL diluent in tubes 2–6, 200 µL of 1000 ng/mL in tube 1, then move 100 µL down the line → 1000, 500, 250, 125, 62.5, 31.25 ng/mL.

      Good to know

      1. Free to use. No subscription. Nothing you type leaves your browser.
      2. Errors add up along a series — mix well and change tips at every step.
      3. Volumes are rounded to 4 significant figures.
