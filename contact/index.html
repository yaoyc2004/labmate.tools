WHAT HELPS

A question or a wrong number

Say which tool, what you typed and what you expected. A screenshot isn't needed — the numbers are enough.

Requesting a tool

Describe the step that slows you down, how you handle it today, and how often it comes up. For example: “I recalculate the antibody dilution for every new lot by hand, about twice a week.”

A qPCR instrument that isn't read

Name the instrument, the software version and the export menu you used. We'll reply and ask for an example export — sample names can be replaced with codes.
