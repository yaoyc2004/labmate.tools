SECTION 01 — PLATESTracking & layout

  • FIG. 1.1 ABCD Plate Tracker Tap each well as you pipette. See what's done, stage by stage, and never lose your place. TAP → TRACK → PASS IN PROGRESS
  • FIG. 1.2 ABCD Plate Layout Map samples and controls to wells, then print a clean layout. PASTE → MAP → PRINT IN PROGRESS

SECTION 02 — CALCULATORSMixes, dilutions, molarity

  • FIG. 2.1 ×n+10% Master Mix Scale a reaction mix for any number of samples, with overage built in. ENTER → SCALE → PRINT IN PROGRESS
  • FIG. 2.2 C1V1 = C2V2 → Dilution Solve for whichever value is missing: concentration or volume, stock or final. ENTER → SOLVE → PIPETTE IN PROGRESS
  • FIG. 2.3 c = mM · V Molarity Move between mass, volume and concentration in any unit. ENTER → CONVERT → WEIGH IN PROGRESS
  • FIG. 2.4 Serial Dilution Plan a 2-fold, 10-fold or any-fold series: diluent and transfer for every tube. SET → PLAN → PIPETTE IN PROGRESS

SECTION 03 — CONVERTERSUnits & spin speeds

  • FIG. 3.1 rpm↕× g RPM ↔ g Convert centrifuge speed to relative force using your rotor radius, and back. ENTER → CONVERT → SPIN IN PROGRESS

SECTION 04 — LABELSTubes, vials & boxes

  • FIG. 4.1 Cryo Labels Turn a list of sample IDs into a print-ready label sheet, lined up to your printer. LIST → LAYOUT → ALIGN → PDF IN PROGRESS

SECTION 05 — CELLS & PROTEINCounting, seeding & loading

  • FIG. 5.1 Cell Count & Seeding Hemocytometer squares to live cells/mL and viability, then volumes to seed any plate or flask. COUNT → SEED → PLATE IN PROGRESS
  • FIG. 5.2 Protein Loading Fit a BCA or Bradford curve, then get equal-µg Western blot lanes: sample, buffer, water. READ → FIT → LOAD IN PROGRESS

SECTION 06 — PRINTPosters & print shops

  • FIG. 6.1 Poster Check Check that your poster file prints at the size you order and fits the board — before you pay for it. FILE → SIZE → CHECK → SEND IN PROGRESS

SECTION 07 — CYTOMETRYPanels, spectra & spillover

  • FIG. 7.1 Panel Designer Assign fluors to markers on your own cytometer and see the estimated spillover before you stain. Conventional and spectral. PICK → ASSIGN → CHECK IN PROGRESS
