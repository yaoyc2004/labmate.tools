labmatetools / PRIVACY ALL TOOLS

Privacy

Short version: no accounts, no tracking, no cookies. What you type into a tool stays in your browser.

THE DETAILSLAST UPDATED OCTOBER 2026

What you type

Every tool runs in your browser. Sample names, volumes, concentrations and plate progress are worked out on your device and are not sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server that receives them.

What your browser keeps

Some tools save your last inputs in your browser's local storage, so a refresh or a closed tab doesn't lose your work — for example the Plate Tracker's plate and the calculators' last values. This stays on that device and that browser only.

  • To remove it, use the tool's CLEAR or RESET button, or clear site data for labmate.tools in your browser settings.
  • In private browsing, nothing is kept after you close the window.

Files you open

When a tool lets you open a file — like a sample list for Cryo Labels — the file is read in your browser. It is not uploaded.

No accounts, analytics or cookies

There is nothing to sign up for. We don't use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies.

Services the pages load

To show the page, your browser fetches a few files from other services. Like any web request, they receive your IP address and browser details, under their own privacy policies:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) serves the PDF and QR-code libraries used by Cryo Labels.

None of these receive what you type into the tools.

Emails

If you write to hello@labmate.tools to request a tool, we use your email only to reply and to understand the request.

Changes

If this ever changes — for example if we add a sponsor or a sign-up — we'll update this page first and change the date above.
